Building rigorous methods for better health decisions.

I’m Guanhua Chen, Associate Professor of Biostatistics and Medical Informatics at the University of Wisconsin–Madison. My group develops statistical and AI methods that turn complex health data into interpretable, equitable, and personalized decisions.

Research

From complex data to decisions that travel.

My work connects statistical theory with consequential biomedical questions. The through-line is reliable personalization: methods that learn who benefits, remain interpretable, and generalize beyond a single dataset.

01

Personalized treatment decisions

Interpretable and fair treatment rules for binary, continuous, censored, and semi-continuous outcomes—built to support real clinical decisions.

02

Clinical AI & informatics

Generalizable multimodal models, auditable language models, and uncertainty-aware clinical AI that remains reliable under dataset shift.

03

Microbiome & multi-omics

Robust association, mediation, and meta-analysis methods that connect microbial communities, host genetics, and disease.

04

Causal generalization

Weighting and balancing strategies that transport evidence across trials, health systems, and diverse patient populations.

Research philosophy

Precision medicine is not only about predicting an outcome. It is about learning which action works best, for whom, with evidence we can trust.
